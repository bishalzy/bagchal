About Baghchal (Tigers vs Goats)

Baghchal (Nepali: बाघचाल) is a traditional, strategic, two-player board game from Nepal. The game is highly asymmetric, meaning the two players have completely different pieces and objectives. One player controls four Tigers (Bagh), and the other player controls twenty Goats (Bakhra).

Objective and Rules

The tigers' goal is to hunt the goats. A tiger can capture a goat by jumping over it to an empty adjacent intersection (similar to checkers). The tigers win if they successfully capture 5 goats.

The goats' goal is to surround and trap the tigers. Goats cannot jump or capture tigers; instead, they win if they block all four tigers so that none of them have a legal move.
