About Baghchal — Tigers vs Goats

Baghchal Online brings the classic experience of the traditional Nepali board game to the digital world. Also known as Bagh Chal ("Move of the Tigers"), Bagchal, or simply Tigers and Goats, this famous Nepali board game is a two-player strategy game where one person plays as four tigers and the other plays as twenty goats.

How to Play Baghchal (Rules)

The Baghchal board is a 5×5 grid with lines connecting the intersection points. Here are the core rules:

  • The Goal: Tigers want to capture 5 goats. Goats want to trap all 4 tigers so they cannot move.
  • Setup: The 4 tigers start in the four corners of the board.
  • Phase 1 (Placement): The goat player places their 20 goats on the board one by one on empty intersections. During this phase, tigers can move one step along the lines and can capture goats by jumping.
  • Phase 2 (Movement): After all 20 goats are placed, goats can finally move one step at a time along the lines.
  • Capturing: Tigers can capture a goat by jumping over it into an empty space beyond it (just like in checkers). Goats cannot jump or capture.

Goat Strategy: How to Win

  • Control the Center: Place your first few goats in the middle of the board to block the tigers early. The center has the most connections.
  • Work as a Team: Keep your goats close together in connected groups. An isolated goat is easy prey for a tiger.
  • Be Patient: Slowly trap the tigers by reducing their available moves. Don't rush — your starting placement is the most important part of the game.

Tiger Strategy: How to Win

  • Hunt the Weak: Look for goats that are alone near the edges or corners and capture them quickly before formations solidify.
  • Keep Moving: Don't let your tigers get stuck in the corners. Keep them in open positions so they have room to jump.
  • Break Formations: If you see the goats building a strong wall, jump over them early before they finish surrounding you.
